## What They Do
Socratix AI provides AI agents that automate fraud and compliance investigations. The platform integrates into existing workflows to gather evidence, analyze threats, and provide explainable decisions, aiming to replace manual alert reviews.

## Team
Strong technical and domain-specific background. Founders have relevant experience at DoorDash, Unit21, Cruise, and LinkedIn, with proven track records in building fraud tooling and scaling enterprise AI systems.

## Market
Targeting fraud and compliance teams at fintechs and banks. The problem of manual, error-prone alert investigation is well-defined and high-stakes, fitting the 'system of action' thesis.

## Why Now
Advancements in LLMs allow for reasoning across unstructured signals and edge cases that traditional ML models struggle with, enabling the transition from simple detection to automated investigation.

- Integration complexity with legacy banking systems could hinder rapid deployment.
- Potential for 'AI hallucination' in compliance-sensitive decision making.
