## What They Do
Fiber AI provides a suite of B2B data APIs and webhooks covering 40M+ companies, 850M+ people, and 30M+ jobs. It positions itself as a low-cost, high-freshness data infrastructure layer for AI-native sales, recruiting, and growth tools, offering unique endpoints like real-time LinkedIn fetching and reverse email lookup.

## Team
The team consists of 8 members. No specific founder background or technical pedigree is provided in the evidence packet.

## Market
The company operates in the crowded B2B data enrichment and sales intelligence market, competing against established players like Apollo, ZoomInfo, and People Data Labs. The value proposition is primarily cost-arbitrage and data freshness.

## Why Now
The proliferation of AI SDR and autonomous sales agents has created a massive demand for high-quality, real-time, and cost-effective data feeds to power these agents, moving beyond static databases.

## Risks
- High dependency on third-party data sources (LinkedIn, Crunchbase, etc.) which may change terms or block access.
- Commoditization risk: Competing against well-funded incumbents (Apollo, ZoomInfo) who may lower prices or improve their own API offerings.
- Lack of proprietary data moat; the business model relies on aggregating and cleaning data from 100+ sources.
- Potential legal/compliance risks associated with scraping and data brokering.

## Open Questions

- What is the actual revenue or growth trajectory beyond the claim of 'helping 100s of enterprises'?
- Who are the founders and what is their prior experience in data infrastructure?
- How does the company ensure long-term data quality and compliance given the reliance on 100+ external sources?

## The Call: Watch
Fiber AI is a functional, utility-driven business that serves a clear need for AI agent builders. However, it functions more as a data commodity provider than a 'system of action' as defined in our thesis. While it has traction, the lack of a clear technical moat and the reliance on third-party data sources make it a 'Watch' rather than a 'Take a meeting' until we see evidence of proprietary data generation or deeper workflow integration.

Thesis fit: It is a critical infrastructure provider for the AI sales/recruiting ecosystem, which aligns with our interest in tools that power lean, automated GTM teams.

What would change our mind:
- Evidence of proprietary data collection that incumbents cannot easily replicate.
- Proof of deeper integration into customer workflows (becoming a 'system of action' rather than just a data pipe).
- Detailed founder background demonstrating unique expertise in data engineering or scraping at scale.
