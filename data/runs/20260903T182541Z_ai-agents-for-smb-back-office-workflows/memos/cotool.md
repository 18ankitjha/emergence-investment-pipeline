## What They Do
Cotool is an agentic security platform designed to automate security operations (SecOps) workflows. It features an AI co-pilot for context assembly, a no-code agent builder for creating custom automations, and automated report generation. It aims to move beyond 'AI-powered insights' to provide a system of action for detection, response, and threat hunting.

## The Call: Watch
Cotool aligns well with our thesis of moving from 'chatbots' to 'systems of action' in high-frequency operational workflows. They have identified a clear pain point and have early traction with mid-market/enterprise-scale users. However, the lack of information on the founding team and the competitive nature of the security automation space warrants a 'Watch' status until more data on technical differentiation and retention is available.

Thesis fit: The product directly addresses the 'system of action' requirement by allowing users to build reusable automations rather than just querying an LLM.

What would change our mind:
- Evidence of high retention or expansion within the existing pilot customers.
- Technical deep-dive showing proprietary agentic architecture vs. standard LLM API wrappers.
- Founder background demonstrating deep domain expertise in cybersecurity.
