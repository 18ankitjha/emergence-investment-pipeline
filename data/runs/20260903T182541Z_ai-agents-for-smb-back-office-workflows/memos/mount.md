## What They Do
Mount is an AI-native insurance carrier focused on liability coverage for deployed AI agents. The product combines security tooling (vulnerability scanning, red-teaming, and automated remediation) with insurance underwriting to provide real-time risk assessment and coverage for AI-specific risks like prompt injection and autonomous workflow errors.

## Market

## Why Now
As businesses transition from human-operated software to autonomous AI agents, legacy insurance policies are often silent or exclusionary, creating a clear demand for purpose-built coverage that integrates directly with technical risk assessment.

- Potential for 'thin wrapper' risk if the security scanning component is easily commoditized by existing cybersecurity vendors.
- Early-stage team size (2) may struggle with the heavy operational and regulatory burden of the insurance industry.
