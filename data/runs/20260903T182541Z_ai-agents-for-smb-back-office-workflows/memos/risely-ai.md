## What They Do
Risely AI provides agentic AI workflows for universities, integrating with existing SIS, LMS, and CRM systems to automate administrative tasks such as student retention, financial aid processing, and enrollment management.

## Team
The team is exceptionally strong for a seed-stage company. The founders bring highly relevant domain expertise (Salesforce Education Cloud), technical scaling experience (DoorDash), and proven GTM/sales execution (Airwallex, NerdWallet).

## Market
The target market is higher education, characterized by a $735 billion annual spend on administrative labor. The market is facing significant budget pressures and staffing shortages, creating a clear pain point for automation.

## Why Now
Universities are facing unprecedented budget and staffing constraints, and agentic AI has reached a maturity level capable of handling the nuanced, autonomous judgment required for complex university workflows.

## Risks
- Long enterprise sales cycles typical of higher education institutions.
- High integration complexity with legacy university systems (SIS/LMS).
- Data privacy and security requirements in an educational context.
- Potential for institutional inertia and resistance to adopting new 'operating systems'.
