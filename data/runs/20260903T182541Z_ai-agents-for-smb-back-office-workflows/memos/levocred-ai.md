## What They Do
Levocred AI provides an 'AI workforce' for lenders, automating back-office tasks, portfolio monitoring, compliance, and underwriting. The platform integrates into existing workflows, such as drafting IC memos from loan tapes and credit agreements, and providing Slack-based Q&A for portfolio monitoring.

## Market
Targeting credit funds, fintechs, and banks. The company claims to be live across multiple lending teams with $1Bn+ in receivables processed, indicating a focus on high-value financial operations.

## Why Now
The proliferation of AI agents allows for the automation of complex, document-heavy financial workflows that previously required significant manual human labor in lending operations.

## Risks
- High concentration risk if the product is dependent on a small number of large institutional clients.
- Regulatory and compliance risks inherent in automating underwriting and credit decisions.
- Small team size (2) may struggle to support complex enterprise-grade integrations and security requirements.
- Potential for 'thin wrapper' risk if the core value proposition is easily replicated by incumbents or general-purpose LLM tools.
