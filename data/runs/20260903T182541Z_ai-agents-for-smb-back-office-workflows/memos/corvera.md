# Corvera

**Take a meeting** · **81/100**
https://corvera.ai/ · YC Winter 2026 · B2B

## What They Do
Corvera provides a 'context layer' for CPG brands, unifying disparate data sources into a format (via MCP) that allows AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor) to access and act upon business data for supply chain and category management workflows.

## Team
Strong technical and operational pedigree. Founders include a 2x founder/ex-CEO, an ex-Google Data & AI lead, and an ex-Head of Product with a Princeton MEng in CS.

## Market
Targeting omnichannel CPG brands at the $100M+ revenue inflection point, aiming to serve the $5.4T global CPG industry.

## Why Now
The rise of Model Context Protocol (MCP) and AI-native tooling (Cursor, Lovable) creates a need for a unified data layer to make business data 'legible' to agents, moving beyond simple chatbot interfaces.

## Risks
- High dependency on the adoption of the Model Context Protocol (MCP) standard.
- Potential for CPG brands to build internal data pipelines rather than relying on a third-party layer.
- Early-stage traction (4 weeks) may not reflect long-term retention or churn.
- Competitive risk from incumbent ERP/BI platforms adding native AI integration layers.

## Open Questions
- What is the long-term retention of the 12 brands acquired in the first 4 weeks?
- How does the product differentiate from existing data integration/ETL tools that also provide API access for LLMs?
- What is the specific pricing model for the $33k MRR?

## The Call: Take a meeting
Corvera fits the thesis perfectly by acting as a system of action for operational workflows rather than a thin wrapper. The team's background and rapid initial traction warrant a deep dive.

Thesis fit: They are building the infrastructure layer that allows SMB/mid-market teams to automate complex CPG workflows, directly addressing our interest in AI-driven operational systems.

What would change our mind:
- Evidence of high churn among the initial 12 customers.
- Discovery that the product is merely a wrapper for existing BI tools without deep workflow automation capabilities.
- Evidence that the $100M+ CPG segment prefers building proprietary data layers over third-party solutions.
