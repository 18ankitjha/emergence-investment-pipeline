## What They Do
Lumari is an AI-powered procurement automation platform that deploys autonomous agents to manage end-to-end supply chain workflows, including RFQ management, PO expediting, and supplier communication. It integrates with existing ERP systems without requiring supplier onboarding or system replacement.

## Team
The team consists of 5 members with engineering backgrounds from Google, Tesla, Amazon, and Stripe, specifically with experience in scaling supply chain systems.

## Market
The platform targets a broad range of customers from fast-growing startups to Fortune 500 companies within the manufacturing and supply chain sectors.

## Why Now
The proliferation of agentic AI frameworks allows for the automation of high-frequency, manual procurement tasks (e.g., email parsing, PO updates) that were previously resistant to traditional software automation.

## Risks
- High integration complexity with diverse legacy ERP systems.
- Potential for AI hallucination or errors in critical supply chain execution.
- Dependency on supplier cooperation and email communication standards.
- Lack of disclosed specific traction metrics or customer logos.
