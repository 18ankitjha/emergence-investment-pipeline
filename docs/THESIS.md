# Thesis

We are looking for seed-stage AI companies that automate high-frequency operational workflows for SMBs or lean mid-market teams, where the product can become a system of action rather than a thin chatbot interface.

This thesis deliberately rejects broad "AI assistant for everyone" products. The pipeline should favor companies with:

- A specific repeated workflow.
- A clear business user or budget owner.
- Evidence that the product executes work, not only answers questions.
- Freshness or traction signals from YC metadata, HN, or the company website.

## Scoring

| Area | Max |
|---|---:|
| Team | 20 |
| Product | 20 |
| Market | 15 |
| Traction/Freshness | 15 |
| Why Now | 10 |
| Defensibility | 10 |
| Risk Adjustment | 10 |

Thresholds:

- 75-100: Take a meeting
- 55-74: Watch
- 0-54: Pass

The final total and recommendation threshold are enforced by deterministic Python, not by the LLM.
